I.

Manifesto

We dress the hours that other houses refuse to acknowledge.

Vespertine is not a fashion house. It is a small, slow atelier devoted to the rite of getting dressed in the dark. We believe a garment can carry grief, intention and silence. We work in lambswool, raw silk, waxed cotton, corded velvet and oxidised brass. Nothing is mass-cut; nothing is hurried.

  • ✞ Black, bone, oxblood, pewter. No accidents of colour.
  • ✞ Hand-finished in numbered editions of forty or fewer.
  • ✞ Repaired for life. We never replace, only mend.
  • ✞ Shipped in unmarked muslin, with a candle and a wax seal.
